Multisell enhancements. Requires [GS 1.1.9] 
Supports taxable multisells and multisells that maintain enchantment. 
SA addition/removal can slowly be changed to multisell with this. 
NOTE 1: I changed prices for duals as per my sources 
NOTE 2: I allowed unsealing armors while maintaining enchantment. This 
means that people will be able to end up with sealed gloves+3 (for example). 
However, I allowed this so that people can unseal those again without loss. 
For example, having unsealed heavy gloves +3, get sealed +3, and then unseal into 
light gloves +3... 
According to my sources, this lossless conversion exists in retail.
